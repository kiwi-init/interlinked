Interlinked

A minimal browser for macOS.

Your tabs run down a sidebar, and each page is its own card with its own header, like the panes of a terminal multiplexer. Nothing floats over the page.

DownloadmacOS 26, Apple silicon Source Code 
brew install --cask kiwi-init/tap/interlinked

Features

Built in

Ad and tracker blocking
Brave's blocking engine with uBlock Origin's lists, inside the browser. No extensions to install.
Clean addresses
Tracking parameters like utm_, gclid and fbclid come off every address before the page loads.
Dark pages
Sites with a dark theme are asked for it, and light pages are inverted, images left as they are.
Tabs that rest
A tab you leave alone sleeps and comes back where you left it, scroll position included.
History on your Mac
Kept for 90 days on this Mac, cleared by the hour, the day or all at once, or turned off.
Your tint
Color the glass around the page, or let it follow your Mac's accent color.

Three espresso machines we keep coming back to

By Ana Duarte · 6 min read

We've pulled a lot of shots this year. Most machines were fine. These three made us look forward to getting up.

Morrow Classic

A heavy steel box that heats up in about twenty minutes and then never lets you down. The steam wand is strong enough for proper microfoam, and the drip tray is big enough that you'll forget it exists. It's loud. We don't mind.

Tamper Mini

Small enough for a studio kitchen and quiet enough for one with a sleeping roommate. It struggles with back-to-back milk drinks, but for one espresso before work it's lovely.

Brio Two

The one we'd buy again tomorrow.

The Brio Two is guaranteed to make the best coffee you have ever tasted, every single time.

Honestly, skip the other two. Full disclosure: Brio is owned by the same company that runs this blog.

Only 3 left. If you actually care about the people you make coffee for, you'll order one today.

Before you buy

Every machine on this list is covered for three full years, parts and repairs included.

Descale every two months if your water is hard, and use a burr grinder. It matters more than the machine.
